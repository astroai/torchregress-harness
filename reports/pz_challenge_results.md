## Conformal methods across all 16 datasets

See `reports/pz_cp_extended_summary.md` for the CP table.  Split, CQR, Mondrian, CV+ and local CP all give 90% coverage 0.899–0.925; CV+ is the most conservative (0.925 mean) and normalized CP has the smallest width on average; CQR has the lowest mean RMSE among the CP variants.

## Extended heads across all 16 datasets

See `reports/pz_cp_extended_summary.md`.  Beta-NLL is the strongest extended probabilistic head (mean RMSE 0.055, NLL-like coverage 0.895); evidential NIG is badly over-covered at 90% (0.997), so its Student-t intervals need recalibration.  Density-weighted MSE is much worse on PZ (mean RMSE 0.091) because the redshift density is strongly concentrated; BMC recovers to 0.058.  The error-aware input-noise head (0.061) is between the plain MLP and the Gaussian/NLL heads, not a replacement for a proper EIV latent model.

## Paper-style removal (task 1 and task 4, held-out test)

Full vs removed-training deltas are in `reports/pz_removal_summary.md`.  Removing the faint third or the reddest 20% of training raises RMSE by 1.3–3.5× on task 4 and 1.5–2.9× on task 1; removing a sky quadrant is milder.  Coverage drops in the omitted-faint case (Gaussian 0.90→0.85 task 1; 0.91→0.79 task 4), qualitatively matching the paper's finding that PDF calibration changes in method-dependent directions.

## Selection/blend shift (task1-trained model → tasksets 2–4)

`reports/pz_ood_summary.md` gives the held-out task-1 model applied to tasksets 2/3/4.  RMSE rises from ~0.026 (task 1) to 0.064/0.113/0.129 (MSE) and TabM stays best at 0.054/0.092/0.106; Gaussian 90% coverage falls from 0.90 to 0.86/0.89/0.87.  This is the closest public-data analogue of the challenge's non-representative tasksets because the official test labels are withheld.

## Normalizing-flow comparison

Proxy flow comparison (same features, same training budget) is generated by `pz_flow`; see `reports/pz_flow_summary.md` once complete.  Paper reference values: cINN σ_NMAD 0.018 / η 1.60%, cNSF 0.017 / 0.83% on CSST; cNSF is ~4× faster in the paper.  Our cNSF has a numerical NLL outlier on task4 flagship 1yr (one extreme density underflow), so we report median/trimmed NLL alongside the mean and flag it as a flow-domain/bound issue.

## n(z) and PPI

`reports/pz_nz_summary.md` is generated from the `nz_challenge` suite.  The suite trains on all 10 DDF fields, predicts the 1M-object WFD p(z), and evaluates against the reserved WFD truth; PPI rectifies mean-z, median-z and tomographic-bin fractions using a small labeled WFD slice plus predictions on the rest.
